Who We Are

Arcade started with a simple idea: give everyone on and around campus a place to grab food that's quick, affordable, and actually tastes like someone cared about it. Every order is prepared fresh, right on campus, so you're never waiting long or settling for less.

From crispy loaded fries and loaded mac piled high with your favourite toppings, to jollof, rice pilaf, fresh juices, and something sweet to finish — we keep things simple, consistent, and made to order — the way campus food should be.
