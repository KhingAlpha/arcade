About Arcade

Who we are, and what goes into every plate.

Our Story

Arcade started as a simple idea: give KNUST students and staff a quick, reliable place to grab food that actually tastes like it was made with care. Every order is prepared fresh, close to campus, so you're never waiting long or settling for less.

Our Loaded Fries

Crispy on the outside, fluffy inside, and piled high with your choice of chicken, sausage, ham, or beef. We finish every plate with melted cheese and a signature sauce, and you can always add extra toppings if you want more.

Our Salads

Cut fresh daily, our salads range from a light veggie bowl to heartier options like the Chef's and Pittsburgh salad, built with real protein and a dressing that doesn't drown out the vegetables.

Where To Find Us

We're based at KNUST, St. Theresa's Hostel. Reach out on WhatsApp or call ahead to place an order, and check our Menu page for full pricing.
